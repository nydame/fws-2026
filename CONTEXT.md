# Firefly Web Services

The website at go-firefly.com: a solo practitioner's site that persuades mission-driven organizations to hire her for freelance web work, and in doing so gives prospective employers evidence of her ability.

## Language

### Who

**Firefly Web Services**:
The trading name Felicia María Betancourt practises under. A shingle over a solo practice, not an organization with staff — so the site always speaks as "I", never "we".
_Avoid_: FWS in prose (fine in filenames), the agency, the company, the team

**Prospective Client**:
Someone evaluating whether to pay for freelance work. The primary audience: every page is written to persuade this reader first.
_Avoid_: customer, lead, visitor

**Prospective Employer**:
Someone evaluating whether to hire her into a role. The secondary audience, served by the same evidence a Prospective Client reads, not by separate content.
_Avoid_: recruiter, hiring manager

**Client**:
An organization she has already done paid work for. Named on the site only where the engagement permits; an unnamed Client still anchors a Project.
_Avoid_: customer, account, employer (a Client hires the practice, not the person)

**Mission-Driven Organization**:
A nonprofit, union, research lab, arts group, or civic project — an organization that exists to do something rather than to make something. Nearly every Client is one, and the practice is aimed at them deliberately.
_Avoid_: nonprofit (too narrow — IBEW and E3SM are neither), NGO, purpose-driven, social good, cause

### What the site shows

**Project**:
One piece of work, shown to demonstrate capability. The unit the portfolio is built from.
_Avoid_: case study (the 2016 site's term for narrative HTML fragments; retired), portfolio piece, sample

**Recent Work**:
Projects from 2018 onward. Leads the portfolio, and carries the argument that she is working now.
_Avoid_: new work, latest, current work

**Earlier Work**:
The pre-2018 work from the 2016-era site, kept as a short dated list for career continuity rather than as full Projects. Demoted, not deleted.
_Avoid_: archive, old work, legacy

**Post**:
A piece of writing published on this site at `/blog/`. Evidence of how she thinks, and the main reason a stranger arrives.
_Avoid_: article, blog (the section is a blog; a single item is a Post)

### What the site collects

**Client Inquiry**:
A message from a Prospective Client sent through the site, asking about paid work. The site's one conversion: everything else exists to produce one.
_Avoid_: contact, submission, lead, enquiry
