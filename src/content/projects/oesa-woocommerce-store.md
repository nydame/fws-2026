One Earth Sacred Arts, a local arts collective, started with WordPress as a way to build a site that would provide one place where people could find and read about members of the group. Later, when they decided to sell their artwork and coloring books online, we tried a few solutions before settling on WooCommerce. Over the years they needed that store to keep working as payment and security standards evolved and as the world moved from desktop to mobile. For the last rebuild, I used a block theme with a fluid-grid and added product filtering. After more than a decade, their combined blog and online store still drives traffic and sales.
