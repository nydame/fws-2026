One Earth Sacred Arts, a small sacred-arts business, needed to sell artwork and coloring books online without an enterprise budget — and then needed that store to keep working as payment, security, and platform standards changed from 2012 through 2024. I built the site on a custom fluid-grid WordPress theme, replaced its original e-junkie/PayPal store with WooCommerce, forced the site onto SSL, and carried it through store revamps and relaunches, later adding artist-attribute product filtering and evaluating modern payment, shipping, and tax integrations. Twelve years on, the store is still selling on a platform that has survived every payment, security, and platform shift since 2012.
