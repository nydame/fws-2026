Rubicon Programs, a reentry-services nonprofit, had two WordPress block-theme sites that didn't match the organization's brand and offered no consistent way to apply it. I created child themes and remapped each theme's palette and typography in theme.json to Rubicon's colors and Helvetica-family fonts — advising on licensed-versus-Google-font tradeoffs — and solved a custom "polaroid" photo-stack effect with synced block patterns and CSS after establishing that block style variations can't target arbitrary blocks. Both sites now carry one coherent brand that staff can maintain themselves in the Site Editor, with the custom effects preserved as reusable patterns.
