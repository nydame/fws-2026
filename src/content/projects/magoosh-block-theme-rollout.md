Magoosh's network of blogs was stuck on a legacy 2019 theme that locked editors out of WordPress's modern block-based editing tools and made every design change a developer task. After a scoping and research phase, I built a new block theme (magoosh2023) as a Twenty Twenty-Three child, defining the brand's palette and typography in theme.json, and rolled it out sub-site by sub-site so the live network was never disrupted. Editors gained direct control over layout and styling, routine design changes stopped requiring a developer, and no sub-site went down during the rollout.
